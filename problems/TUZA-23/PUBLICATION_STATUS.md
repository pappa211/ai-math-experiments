## Provenance and review limits

Historical files carry a curation banner. Private paths and operational identifiers were removed; immutable original and public SHA-256 values are mapped in the source manifest. Exact mathematical Lean sources and dependency pins are retained. Byte-identical inherited snapshots use relative symlinks to canonical public files. Historical launch scripts may retain placeholder paths or expired deadlines and are not current portable entry points.

All reviews are internal AI reviews, not external human specialist peer review. No priority claim or new license is introduced. Fresh publication checks are separately indexed in [verification](../../verification/tuza-20-26/README.md); old receipts are inherited evidence only.
