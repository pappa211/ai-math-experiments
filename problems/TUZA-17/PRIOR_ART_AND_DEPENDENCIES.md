## Published mathematical inputs

1. **Ron Aharoni, “Ryser's conjecture for tripartite 3-graphs,” Combinatorica 21 (2001), 1–4**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s004930170001). The used statement is tau(T)<=2nu(T) for every finite tripartite 3-uniform hypergraph. The triangle-edge colours of H partition the vertices (graph edges) of its full triangle hypergraph; deleting graph edges preserves this hypothesis. Publisher preview was accessible, original subscription PDF was not. The precise inequality and its derivation were inspected in HNS Section 3, printed pp.8–9. We do not claim a new proof of Aharoni or a full independent audit of its topological machinery.

2. **Penny Haxell, Lothar Narins and Tibor Szabó, “Extremal Hypergraphs for Ryser's Conjecture,” Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 158 (2018), 492–547**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2018.04.004), [author manuscript dated 18 March 2018](https://page.mi.fu-berlin.de/szabo/PDF/ryser.pdf). Theorem 1.1 characterizes equality tau=2nu in tripartite 3-graphs as home-base hypergraphs. The inspected interface comprises Theorem 1.1, Definitions 7.1–7.4 and Proposition 7.5, especially the auxiliary matchings yielding disjoint loose-cycle supports. The downloaded exact PDF and its SHA256 are retained; pp.1,3,8,9,19,20,21 were read, and selected statement/definition pages rendered. The full 56-page deep proof was **not independently reproved**.

   The graph-specific extraction of K4s was already present in pinned TUZA-01, and the exhaustion step in TUZA-03. These were read, reconstructed and checked by the fresh critic. For a triangle-edge-3-colourable graph J with tau(J)=2nu(J)=2k, the full actual triangle family consists exactly of the faces of k edge-disjoint K4s; blocks may share vertices and there may be edges in no triangle. This is the only equality classification used in the new AC arguments. It does not apply to arbitrary tight graphs. The exact external-to-project-to-new-claim dependency chain is preserved in PROOFS_AND_OBSTRUCTIONS.md.

3. **Gallai's factor-critical matching lemma**, inspected through [Alexander Schrijver's one-page CWI exposition](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~lex/files/tutteb.pdf) (undated file; inspected 4 November 2026). The used statement says a connected graph in which every vertex is missed by some maximum matching is factor-critical. A complete elementary reconstruction is in notes/A_ELEMENTARY_MATCHING_BLOCKER.md and the proof compilation. The fresh critic checked the extremal-matching argument against the actual page. The final critical-nonsaturation proof therefore does not depend on an unproved invocation of Tutte–Berge. Initial alternative notes referenced Tutte–Berge and Penev's 2022 lecture notes; those are not needed in the final proof chain. An unfinished proof in Tamitegama's 2018 notes is not used as proof authority.

4. **Freitas, “A conjectura de Tuza sobre triângulos em grafos,” 2014 thesis**, [primary institutional record](https://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/9599?mode=full). Its institutional abstract explicitly states that a minimum counterexample is 4-connected. The PDF download timed out; its full proof was not inspected. The mission independently reconstructs the stated separator implication, but does not claim novelty for that conclusion. The two-edge adhesion ingredient was already in TUZA-11 and overlaps prior Krivelevich work. [Krivelevich's 1995 author PDF](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~krivelev/2.pdf), Lemma 5, was inspected for the two-vertex separator context; no new decisive argument depends on an unseen proof there.

5. **Gregory Puleo, arXiv:1510.07017v4, 6 March 2017**, [primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/1510.07017v4), Theorem 1.9: the join of an independent set with a triangle-free graph satisfies Tuza. The relevant statement was inspected. The strengthened Track A examples also have an explicit cover below twice their exact packing number, so that theorem is attribution/context, not a necessary proof input. The earlier mad<7 inequality is likewise prior Puleo, as recorded by the published project ledger.

6. **Haxell–Narins, “A Stability Theorem for Matchings in Tripartite 3-Graphs,” Combinatorics, Probability and Computing 27 (2018), 774–793**, [arXiv:1701.06451](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.06451), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548318000147). It is **not an input** and its full proof was not inspected.

Classical finite matching and elementary graph facts are stated/proved where needed. The blow-up extension uses a short Hall argument included in full.
