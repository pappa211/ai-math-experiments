This is a verification inventory, not an endorsement of every archived claim. “Accepted ordinary proof” means fresh reconstruction of the complete argument and checked scope of its published inputs. “Conditional Lean” means a completed kernel-checked deduction with explicit published-theorem parameters; those inputs are not discharged by its axiom list. “Not independently checked” is distinct from incomplete or refuted. Full source snapshots and hashes are retained privately. Exact accepted statements are in the manuscript; the source register below prevents unselected claims from disappearing into an aggregate success claim.

Conventions: G is finite simple; T1,T2 are edge-disjoint actual triangles; H=G−(E(T1)∪E(T2)); h=nu(H); D(H) is the full triangle hypergraph. F_i=D(H+E(T_i))−D(H). Tripartite always describes D(H), not a graph-vertex coloring.
