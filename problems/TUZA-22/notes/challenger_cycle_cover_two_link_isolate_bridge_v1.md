# A two-link consistency bridge for cycle-cover obstructions

Status: ORDINARY_CONDITIONAL_BRIDGE, frozen before the authorized fixed-subdomain diagnostic. The logical implication below is ordinary; exclusion of the supplied one-isolate link domain is a separate obligation and is NOT assumed proved here.

Let G be a minimum-CS failure in the hereditary ANY one-cycle-cover class. The accepted reductions make a selected cover C a Hamiltonian cycle and make G K5-free. Every whole vertex link is connected and, after deleting its two selected neighbors, is a path forest of matching number at most two with at most one isolate. The matching bound retains its declared P6 finite premise.

Fix adjacent vertices h,v in G. Suppose in the full actual link L_h the vertex v has degree exactly two, with neighbors u,w, and uv,vw are BOTH selected edges of C. Then u,w are precisely v's two cycle neighbors. Suppose also uw is absent.

In the FULL link L_v, the neighbors of h are exactly the common G-neighbors of h and v. The degree-two hypothesis in L_h therefore says

    N_(L_v)(h)={u,w}.

Consequently h is an isolated vertex of F_v=L_v-{u,w}. The two apices u,w are nonadjacent by hypothesis. The accepted at-most-one-isolate rule means F_v has exactly this one isolate, and its other components are one of the seven nontrivial path forests from challenger_cycle_cover_residual_domain_v1.md. If there were no nontrivial component, L_v would have only three vertices, already excluded by robustness. The full link L_v is K4-free, connected, and has minimum degree at least two.

Therefore exclusion of the EXACT one-isolate/no-apex-edge subdomain would imply that no such configuration h,v,u,w occurs in a minimum failure. In particular, any INTERNAL vertex of a nontrivial F_h path whose only L_h neighbors are its two path neighbors would be forbidden: those two edges are selected cycle edges, and the two neighbors are nonadjacent because F_h is an actual path forest.
