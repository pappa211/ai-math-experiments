Accounting gives p=m+1,tau=2m+1, and resilience excludes zero or one active restored edge. For adjacent missing edges, disjoint-page compatibility forces a single common apex. Resilience forces the old closing edge, hence an old triangle in one clique block. A K5 block would already cover all new pages at old cost, while a K4 gives the literal K5-e core. The clarified absence of ay follows from the old triangle axy, which would join a to the old K4 and force a missing restored edge. A supported fourth spoke ax gives the exact old-cover overlap and is therefore background. Shared-edge connectivity then rules out other old components.

For disjoint restored edges, an all-disjoint pair-family would have one opposite-spoke K4 obstruction. Old-triangle closure and whole-base footprints force singleton families already covered at old cost. Hence an intersecting pair exists. Any outside terminal apex would be compatible with that pair, so all new triangles lie on four terminals. Three old cross edges give two new triangles with one common old edge, contradicting resilience; all four give a K4. Old blocks meet this K4 in at most one edge. The supported subset of its cross four-cycle is nonempty, has no opposite pair, and hence consists of one edge or two adjacent edges. Shared-edge connectivity implies precisely those one or two old blocks and no others. Thus p<=5.

The sharp K4 with two K5s attached on adjacent root edges has exactly the displayed actual triangle family. Deleting the specified opposite root edges leaves two old B blocks. The five-packing and nine-cover are valid; an eight-cover would have to be minimum on both K5s and use only their two root attachment edges, which miss the opposite root face. For every edge deletion, the root triangle and each K5 packing can be chosen to avoid the relevant attachment/deleted edge as stated, preserving five triangles. No extra crossing triangle is created by the fresh-vertex gluing.

The theorem bounds only this classified two-edge-B representation. It does not establish its availability for arbitrary connected resilient defect-one graphs, and the bound is not claimed for vertex-connected unions. No finite computation, global CS hypothesis or external matching theorem is used.
