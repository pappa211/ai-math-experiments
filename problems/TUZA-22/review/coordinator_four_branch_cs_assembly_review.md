The matching contributes at most one surviving edge, and the rim triangle contributes at most one because one of its edges was deleted. A disjoint pair would require four link vertices in one block. Therefore the full footprint is at most a two-edge star, and balanced accounting plus ordinary clique avoidance yields the claimed augmentation. Closedness and selecting all removed rim edges account for arbitrary outside pages.
