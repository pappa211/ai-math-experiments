# Independent review of induced-list even-cycle boundaries

Verdict: ACCEPT `notes/even_cycle_induced_list_boundary_v1.md` at its exact-packing scope.

For C4, an induced apex support is empty, one edge, a two-edge star, or the entire four-cycle. With no universal apex, the minimum-cover inequalities are exactly SDR Hall inequalities. With two universal apices the two opposite matchings give four pages. With exactly one, the remaining supported edges meet every adjacent pair of bases, hence contain an opposite pair; no nonuniversal apex can support both. The displayed four-packing therefore exists in every case.

For each C_(2r), r>=3, the stated neighborhoods induce exactly one three-edge path and 2r-2 two-edge paths. Every actual triangle has one apex and one core edge. Two triangles intersect only consecutively around the listed C_(4r-1): same-apex intersections use incident base edges, and different-apex intersections use a common base. The end pages of the three-edge path are disjoint. Thus the packing number is 2r-1. Every graph edge occurs in at most two of the 4r-1 triangles, so the cover number is at least 2r, attained by the core. All counts and the r=3 literal witness agree with this argument.

The examples refute exact private packing of all minimum core edges already at C6, even with induced lists. They are tripartite, satisfy strict Tuza, and do not refute a factor-two statement or the forest theorem.
