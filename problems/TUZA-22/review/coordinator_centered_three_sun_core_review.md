# Independent review: local centered-three-sun condition and kernel

ACCEPTED, 2026-11-16, for notes/centered_three_sun_core_localization_v1.md. The coordinator reconstructed the local version after separately reviewing the full claw-free proof.

The local hypothesis forbids claws centered at triangles containing the chosen hub a with both other vertices in one closed link component. Three-list closure places b,c,x,y in that same component. The isolation tests can all keep a in their centers: abc for core edges, abx/aby/acx/acy for spokes, and axy for the optional apex edge. Thus the K5-e/K5 component argument is genuinely local and does not assume absence of claws elsewhere. After its exclusion, the same low-codegree pairing forces a friendship graph or cycle in the chosen component, contradicting link deletion.

A central triangle with three pairwise edge-disjoint page triangles is exactly the stated noninduced six-vertex three-sun. All apices are outside the center triangle and distinct. Adding other graph edges does not spoil that witness or its induced claw in the triangle-intersection graph. Therefore the central-vertex operator is monotone under arbitrary graph subgraphs, and its iterated induced restriction is monotone. Every class-minimum CS failure is fixed by the operator. The hereditary-base localization follows without claiming preservation of individual optima at each deletion.

Alternating this operator with the high-codegree core operator also gives a monotone finite restriction that fixes every minimum failure; either threshold can be used only after its own local theorem is admitted. Empty centered-three-sun kernel includes K5; K6 is a nonempty fixed point, so there is no unrestricted emptiness inference.
