# Independent review: apex-unicyclic WKE construction

Verdict: ACCEPT at the stated matching scope, with the declared dependencies retained for CS corollaries. Primary notes/apex_unicyclic_wke_v1.md, SHA d0fb7564463def15f0f77f9713aabfcfcb0437764cf1b9e19bf2dc8f848d968e. Reviewer: challenger, 2026-11-16 UTC. No computation or source import was used.

I independently reconstructed the two induction lemmas. For a leaf u with neighbor v in a pseudoforest, every maximum matching matches v. After deleting u,v and that one matching edge, the remaining matching has size one less and is maximum: a larger remaining matching could be combined with uv. This remains true when v was matched to a third vertex t; t becomes exposed but no extra matching edge disappears. Adding v to the inductive cover covers all edges lost through the deletion. The terminal cycle formulas are exact: even complements are matchings; odd complements are a two-edge path and isolated edges. Hence EVERY maximum matching of a pseudoforest has a complement cover no larger than itself.

For the exposed-leaf lemma, assuming every original leaf is always saturated makes the edge uv compulsory for a selected original leaf u. A nonperfect component after deleting u,v must exist. An exposed leaf of that component either was original, contradicting the assumption immediately, or has lost neighbor v; exchanging uv for vz exposes u. An isolated component has the same adjacency by connectedness. If the component is an odd cycle, connectedness supplies a cycle neighbor z of v, and the cycle matching can expose z before the same swap. These exhaust the inductive alternatives, including disconnected deletion residuals.

The three WKE constructions then check literally. In the nonperfect case the exposed original F-leaf is adjacent to w by delta(L)>=2, while an odd cycle can expose any specified neighbor of w. Appending wz increases matching size by one, paying for the extra covering vertex w. In the perfect noncycle case, F has2r edges and a vertex of degree at least three; removing its perfect matching leaves r edges with an adjacent pair, coverable with at most r-1 vertices. In the even-cycle case, independence of N(w) means every edge in the complementary perfect matching has at most one required endpoint, so choosing one endpoint per edge can include ALL neighbors of w.

The exception is exactly an even cycle F with adjacent w-neighbors. The note correctly treats this as an uncovered class, not an assertion that every such L is non-WKE. The rim-four universal-apex wheel is a genuine boundary and is not silently regarded as forest remainder.

If L is triangle-free, adjacent w-neighbors are impossible, so the ordinary WKE statement covers the full triangle-free apex-unicyclic family. Passing from WKE to a minimum-CS exclusion uses the separately accepted triangle_free_wke_link_exclusion_v1.md and its Kotzig premise. The further one-cycle-cover leaf statements also retain their earlier localization and four-branch finite/declared-input dependencies. The present matching theorem itself is ordinary and self-contained.

No triangle-bearing CS exclusion is inferred merely from WKE. Its residual clique footprints and the exceptional even-wheel branch remain separate obligations exactly as stated. The matching witnesses additionally satisfy the pointed property needed by the forest anchor when they meet w, but no broader anchor theorem is asserted here.
