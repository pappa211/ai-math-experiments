# Independent coordinator review of the residual packing theorem and guardrail

ACCEPTED for notes/challenger_tree_anchor_residual_packing_v2.md and notes/challenger_anchor_residual_realization_boundary_v1.md, 2026-11-16. The parity mechanism was developed jointly; this records the coordinator's separate full reconstruction of the frozen argument. The residual COVER bound remains open.

For any residual packing Q, the P/Q intersection graph only uses nonselected edges, has maximum degree two, and every two-selected-edge triangle has degree at most one. A cycle or a path with two two-selected-edge endpoints consumes every nonselected edge twice. The symmetric difference of its actual triangles is then a nonempty Eulerian subgraph of the selected forest: selected edges cannot cancel between P and Q because P's are deleted in the residual, and they cannot repeat within either packing. This is impossible. Q-Q path endpoints would augment P; a balanced P2-Q1 path would reduce its secondary p2 objective. In every surviving component the Q count is bounded by the number of P1 vertices. Thus nu(R)<=p1, without a cover claim.

The six-vertex K4 residual control is literal and its packing upper bound checks; it proves that actual edge-cover/packing equality need not hold. Its triangle-intersection graph is perfect, so that different property must not be described as failing.

The universal residual construction is also exact. A cover D disjoint from a spanning tree cover C_R receives a separate degree-two outside vertex per base. The selected tree extends by one spoke per new vertex. Those added triangles are disjoint, while D covers every old and new triangle, proving both optima equal |D|. They are all one-selected-edge triangles, so the chosen packing has minimum possible p2=0. Removing its selected spokes leaves exactly R's original triangles plus triangle-free pendant edges. Consequently even arbitrary residual structure is compatible with this extremal packing quantifier; only a budget-relative covering statement could prove the anchored target. The nine-vertex wheel example gives an imperfect C5 residual at p1=tau(R)=3 and does not refute existential anchored choice.
