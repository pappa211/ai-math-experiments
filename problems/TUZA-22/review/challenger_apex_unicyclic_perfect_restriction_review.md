The r hub triangles on M are disjoint and deleted in full. The one-unit-saving classification is exact. A degree-three vertex in R saves at least two compared with choosing one endpoint per edge. Their nonnegative savings add, so saving exactly one permits precisely one P3, P4 or K3 component, with all other nonempty components isolated edges. In the noncycle F branch an adjacent pair necessarily exists after removing the perfect matching, giving equality rather than merely the lower bound.
