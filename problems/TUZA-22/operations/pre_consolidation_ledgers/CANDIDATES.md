## Current admitted and exploratory cover routes

The forest-cover program is now a reviewed scoped theorem: any acyclic triangle-edge cover suffices for CS, with only K4 equality components. Reasons for the initial approach were that a selected leaf exposes exact link geometry, and balanced minimality supplies literal residual blocks. Its two risks were the nonperfect forest matching branch and arbitrary outside-page footprints; both are addressed by the self-contained v2 proofs and fresh reviews. Global forest-cover availability is still false in general and unproved for any useful unrestricted reduction.

The triangle-and-matching minimum-cover program now has a reviewed all-size inequality and a reviewed all-q2 equality theorem. The precise mixed equality question is whether q3 components can survive a minimum CS obstruction. Reasons to pursue it: the weighted conflict proof supplies a strict low-degree alternative, and a degree-three q3 core has an exact balanced four-core reduction. Risks: minimum-cover shape is not hereditary under arbitrary edge deletion, and direct equality arguments can misuse singleton book pages or omit outside footprints. The current intended conclusion is a restriction on a class-minimum CS failure using authenticated M0/codegree, not an unproved direct CS theorem for the whole nonhereditary cover-shape class. A decisive falsifier would be a literal failure of the two-page avoidance or residual-footprint lemma, or a missing triangle in the balanced-star accounting.

The one-cycle-cover route has a reviewed inequality, while equality remains open. It uses the proved forest theorem after deleting one cycle edge. A leaf of any selected cover must see a selected cycle in a minimum CS failure; with exactly one cycle this forces a precise induced-cycle and neighborhood structure. Reasons to pursue stability are its explicit defect-one forest residual and its all-size single-cycle parameter. Risks are the gap between an arbitrary forest cover and an optimum forest cover, and the failure of generic apex-unicyclic WKE already on the wheel over C4. Both are retained as actual auxiliary obstructions, not suppressed assumptions.
