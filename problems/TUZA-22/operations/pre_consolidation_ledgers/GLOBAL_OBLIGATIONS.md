## Additional exact restrictions and surviving gaps

Every pair of edge-disjoint triangles in a minimum CS failure extends to a maximum packing. Thus every triangle of each triangle residual lies in a maximum residual packing. This does not establish extension for larger prescribed packings. A connected edge-resilient defect-one graph with any B triangle residual has packing number at most seven, sharply; minimum-CS triangle residuals in fact cannot have such a B deletion by two-triangle restoration.

The independent-side cover formula has an essential triangle-free subset restriction. Ordinary minimum-cover optimality does not imply private-family minimum-cover optimality; the new literal counterexample rules out that shortcut. Local star-Hall conditions are also insufficient. A global packing theorem would need to exploit the full restriction, actual endpoint realization, and compatible exchanges. The complete-cross independent-side theorem supplies a strict bound only when that shape is present.

A minimum cover's connected components give an exact bipartite path/cycle conflict representation for arbitrary local packings. The tempting sufficient weighted local bound fails on K5-e. Simultaneous cover replacements between components remain a new exploratory question, with no availability theorem.
