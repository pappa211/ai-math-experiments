## Strongest exact results

The cleanest self-contained obstruction is **PROVED_ORDINARY**: choose a triangle-free base B of chromatic number greater than r and order b; take the cone over B[t]. Any r-partition has at leastt² monochromatic rim edges, while every triangle uses two ofbt apex spokes, so2nu<=bt. For t>b no such deletion can be paid, even if its residual cover were free. All edges lie in triangles and the graphs themselves satisfy Tuza. For r4, B=M(M(C5)),t24 gives553 vertices and the exact comparison **576>552**. Its41448 edges and40896 triangles, plus explicit packing/cover witnesses, were independently reconstructed. The witnesses are not claimed optimal.

The strongest amplification limitation has exact **PROVED_ORDINARY** transfer and a **PROVED_USING_PUBLISHED_INPUT** consequence. If X projects homomorphically to G and its edges partition into r label-respecting copies, then tau(X)=rtau(G) and nu*(X)=rnu*(G), including every crossing triangle. The authenticated uniform graph-packing approximation makes nu(X) approach that fractional value at dense scale. The16-vertex GF4 partial-slope example has12 planted K4 copies but an explicit20-packing, with tau=nu*=24 and20<=nu<=21. This defeats unconditional integral replication; it is not a Tuza violation.

The B identity is a valid unbounded scoped ingredient: if independent twins X have common neighborhood S and G[S] has a proper edge colouring into X, deleting X and the q rim edges gives nu(G)=q+nu(R), tau(G)=q+tau(R). Its underlying payment is established reduction methodology. It neither provides a new global move nor proves one must occur. An optional ordinary LP argument also proves tau<=2nu*−1 whenever a triangle exists; this adapts Krivelevich's proof and makes no originality claim.

## What still separates this work from unrestricted Tuza

There is no winning positive route. For compression, the missing global theorem would have to show that every hypothetical minimal counterexample admits one of a specifically defined collection of counterexample-preserving moves. This run has no such collection with a credible existence argument. A decreasing potential alone is insufficient. For amplification, no construction outside the proved copy interfaces has the required integral packing upper bound. For colour repair, no certified payment mechanism covers all obstructions.

There is therefore no evidence that the remaining global theorem is more approachable than Tuza directly. B's exact identities and C's new deductions within this mission are useful scoped results, but neither merits a positive architecture-advance label. Historical originality remains unestablished.
