## Clarification of the rigidity route

The phrase “global R is sufficient” in TUZA-26’s historical proof-chain summary must be read with an explicit class. Let **R12** assert that every graph whose nonempty triangle support is connected through shared graph edges, which is single-edge packing-resilient and has defect `2nu-tau=1`, has triangle support isomorphic to a member of the frozen C12 family. R12, together with the archived strict C12 restoration theorem, is a sufficient route to CS: a hypothetical minimum CS failure supplies a single-triangle residual with those properties, while strict restoration excludes every C12 support. No converse is asserted.

The broader **R-F** candidate in [the TUZA-25 family freeze](../TUZA-25/notes/residual_family_freeze.md) permits additional triangle/K4-overlap gluings. That file explicitly leaves its restoration bridge incomplete. R-F is not silently identified with R12, and this archive does **not** claim that R-F alone implies CS or Tuza. Neither rigidity candidate is proved. This clarification changes no accepted local theorem or finite certificate.
