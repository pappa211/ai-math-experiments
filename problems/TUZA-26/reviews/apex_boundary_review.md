The actual-region definition is essential and correct: F is the COMPLETE set of D edges appearing in an actual triangle not wholly inside the literal K4/K5 D. An outside triangle uses at most one D edge; two such edges would put all its vertices in D. Deleting E(D) minus F therefore preserves every outside triangle and eliminates every internal D triangle when |F|<=2. This gives exactly the K triangle family asserted, with all vertices/background retained.

The packing identity nu(H)=r+nu(K) is exact in the allowed cases. Any packing partitions into at most r D triangles and at most nu(K) outside triangles. Conversely a maximum D packing avoiding all F combines with any maximum K packing. This remains true after deleting ANY surviving K edge g, including g in F, because the chosen D packing already avoids all F. Single-edge resilience consequently passes from H to K. The exception for opposite K4 boundary edges is necessary: K4 minus an opposite pair has no triangle.

I independently reconstructed both cover formulas. For K5, a minimum four-edge clique cover can contain ANY pair F, while every H cover uses at least four D edges and its non-D part covers K-F. Thus tau(H)=4+tau(K-F). For K4 with F nonempty and adjacent when of size two, an outside cover selecting zero, one, or two F edges needs respectively two, one, or one further D edges. Equivalently, choose one f in F, a minimum D cover through f, and a cover of K-f. A lower-bound decomposition of an arbitrary H cover gives the reverse inequality, including covers that select both adjacent F edges. Hence tau(H)=2+min_f tau(K-f), exactly as stated. F empty gives ordinary additivity.

Thus buv is an ACTUAL outside triangle using the D edge uv, forcing uv into the COMPLETE F and contradicting |F|=2.
