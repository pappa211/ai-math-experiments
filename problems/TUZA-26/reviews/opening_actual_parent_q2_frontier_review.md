# Independent q=2 normal form and common-center A payments

Status: ACCEPTED ORDINARY at the stated individual-family and pair/cube interfaces. No complete remaining-family classification or availability assertion is accepted.

Ordinary pages are edge-disjoint exactly when their bases and apices are both distinct: the three bases are mutually adjacent, while all apices are outside the restored terminal triple. The central triangle cannot join another new page in a packing. Hence the family packing number two is exactly the incidence matching number. With two active bases their apex sets are disjoint, since a common apex forces all three pages. With three active bases, Hall deficiency is either a two-base neighborhood of size one or a total neighborhood of size at most two. Actual graph closure forces an apex incident to two bases to be full. This gives precisely A, B or C as stated, including arbitrary-size A/B apex sets and the nonempty extra-book requirement in B.

For a shared pair, a type-A cover using both shared-hub spokes covers every mixed triangle. Choosing an old minimum cover for the other family's cost-two increment, including the exceptional supported-spoke overlap when necessary, then gives complete pair cover gain at most four. Thus A cannot omit its opposite base. No mixed triangle is omitted in this payment.

In a common-center cube with three A families, each omits one hub spoke and its two active bases cover its complete individual family. Any unhit mixed rim has both endpoints among the three omitted-spoke leaves. An old minimum cover leaves a triangle-free graph on those three vertices, hence a star or empty graph. One additional restored hub spoke pays every remaining mixed triangle, giving cover gain at most seven. This is a complete cover saving, not the previously invalid unconstrained three-family sum.

The asymmetric activation warning is correct. An old bare opposite U-page can acquire supported spokes through a restored V opposite-base page; an old bare hub U-page can acquire support through a V hub page at the same external apex. The accepted no-R-bare property therefore does not imply no-B-bare. The source does not assert that these local activations extend to a full actual-parent control or that they settle the remaining A/B/C cases.
