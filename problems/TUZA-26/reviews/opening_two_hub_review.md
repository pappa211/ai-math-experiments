# Independent unbounded two-hub neighborhood review

Status: ACCEPTED ORDINARY at the exact supplied-neighborhood and conditional minimum-Q hypotheses. No finite census, optimizer, or Lean check is involved. The two source scopes are deliberately kept separate.

## All-t exact neutral interface

Deleting cx,cy removes every c-triangle, so Q two-edge resilience forces nu(J)=k. A J cover extends with those two spokes, and a minimum Q cover containing cxu can contain exactly the two c-spokes cx,cu. Its old part contains xu and every yw for w different from u. This gives tau(J)=2k-3, joint minimum-cover inclusion of each opposite pair, and exact defect-one R after deleting that pair. Appending disjoint c-pages proves the packing equalities. Every link edge is critical in J. These are actual deletion statements and do not name J, whose defect is three.

The forbidden configurations for t>2 are whole hub stars, not arbitrary two-edge subsets. Neither source imports the two-leaf forbidden-pair lemma.

## Absent-hub fragile repair

A bare ordinary xu-page has apex p outside the entire neighbor link. Each of its background spokes needs a second Q triangle, and neither the disjoint restored base nor c can supply one. The remaining restored edge f must share an endpoint with both spokes; as f is not xu or an old spoke, it is incident to p. The two required f-pages give old edges xq,uq. Repeating at a bare yv-page forces its apex r to be p or q. In either case the retained edge xv completes an actual old triangle through a bare spoke. The contradiction is valid with arbitrary outside vertices and background. This proves every opposite R resilient when xy is absent, uniformly for all t at least two.

## Present hub with at least five leaves

At least three unmarked independent book apices remain in R. A fragile deletion other than xy leaves two such pages and cannot yield B, so f=xy. For an old clique supporting any unmarked spoke, the actual internal xy-page fixes that whole footprint, and the other restored base contributes at most one adjacent footprint. Every c-page and mixed restored-base triangle was counted. The complete boundary theorem therefore makes all unmarked spokes background. The three ordinary pages of a maximum J packing cannot use them all, contrary to their individual criticality. If R is resilient, all book pages lie in its named active component, but C12 has no edge with three independent common neighbors. The source's elementary construction proof of that fact is valid. Thus the full present-hub t>=5 branch is excluded.

## Complete resilient-R reconstruction

For absent xy, every retained link edge is supported: an ordinary M-page has external apex and uses none of these edges, so a background retained link edge would have Q codegree one. The complete adjacent boundary removes any tight component on an unmarked spoke, placing those spokes in C. Two unmarked leaves give an induced C4, excluding t>=4.

For t=3 the only possible tight pieces lie on xv or yu. Their boundaries include that actual c-page edge and at most one footprint at each end. K4 with at most three boundary edges, and K5 with a P4 boundary, are already paid. A remaining K5 has the indicated triangle boundary. Its two private vertices are outside A and outside C because it already shares x (or y) with C. They cannot be shared with the other exceptional piece: that would create an actual restored-base page through a private edge, contradicting the COMPLETE boundary. No other old component remains, and any newly supported background edge at a private vertex would likewise create a page through a private edge. These are genuinely fresh K5 triangle leaves, so are excluded. The remaining all-C terminal graph is the literal induced P5, impossible in C12.

For present xy, the unmarked book lies in C whenever it has two apices. If it has only one, a hypothetical tight book component has complete boundary exactly within triangle xyw; other tight pieces have at most two adjacent boundary edges and are removed. K4 is paid. A K5 shares at most one vertex with C, so at least one of its two private vertices lies outside C, belongs to no other old component, and cannot gain a new page without enlarging the complete boundary. It is a genuine degree-four simplicial vertex. The independent simplicial theorem excludes it. This carefully requires only one fresh vertex, not unjustified freshness of both.

After the book is in C, the same boundary argument removes every other tight piece. External M-page spokes are in C by codegree two. A marked terminal outside C would have only the other two hub neighbors and c, violating minimum degree four. Thus all terminals lie in C. For t>=4, xy has two independent common neighbors while both endpoints miss a marked C vertex, contradicting the exact C12 fact that such an edge has a universal endpoint.

For t=3 the five terminals induce a bull. The placement classification is correct: the universal root is outside the bull; in a central-star model a triangle in a private construction clique cannot support two pendant vertices at distinct triangle vertices, so the triangle is the central K3 and the two pendants lie in distinct corresponding leaves. W5, K5-e, and the two-K5 model cannot induce a bull. The central cover {rx,yw}, a minimum cover through rx in its leaf, and a minimum cover through yu in the other leaf give a minimum C cover containing yu,yw. Only the selected central attachment rx contributes a cover overlap. Adding xu,yv,cx hits every Q triangle, including arbitrary external M-pages, at cost 2k-2. This is an actual cover saving.

The newly used no-induced-P5 and adjacent-common-neighbor properties of all frozen C12 models were independently reconstructed from their constructions in this review. They are ordinary finite-family proofs, not new enumeration results. The existing induced-C4-free fact is consistent with the same construction argument.

## Composition, remaining cases, and strategy

Every supplied absent-hub K2,t link with t>=3 is excluded by the fragile repair plus complete resilient reconstruction. With the previously accepted t=2 C4 case, the absent-hub family is fully excluded for all t>=2. Present-hub t>=5 is fully excluded, and every resilient opposite residual is excluded for all t>=3. The present-hub fragile cases t=3,4 remain OPEN in these two sources. The t=2 diamond was separately accepted and does not close those cases.

This is a useful unbounded-degree reduction with explicit packing and cover payment. It does not assert that an arbitrary minimum Q has a two-hub neighborhood. After the remaining bounded fragile cases, the decisive further obligation is a source of an actual operation in a general core. One precise discriminator remains joint release of a single outside packed triangle in the two actual endpoint links, with the same hub reserved. The previously accepted release operation would then augment. Whole-triangle minimum covers and the full resilient defect-two predecessor profile have not yet been proved to force that release, or a two-hub neighborhood. No density or low-degree claim follows here.
