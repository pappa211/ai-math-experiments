# Independent actual-parent J0/J1 classification review

Status: ACCEPTED ORDINARY at the exact supplied low-gain two-triangle interface, with arbitrary background retained. This is a constructive support classification, not naming by proper CS or by minimum-Q order. No numerical check or Lean claim is made.

For J0, deleting a book base kills that whole family and leaves at most m+1, so single-edge resilience forces both full K4 families. With equal apices, every touched old piece contains the common apex and exactly one other new-core vertex. Distinct old components cannot share a second vertex; their remaining vertices are outside the core. With different apices, connected triangle support requires an old component touching both new families. The absent core pairs force its two footprints to be ax and ay, so it contains the old edge xy. Any other piece on one arm shares only its hub with this central old piece. Opposite-arm pieces sharing an additional private vertex p would create the old triangle xyp across components, which is impossible. The listed missing core edges also exclude hidden intersections with the opposite new K4. Thus the asserted fresh edge-gluing order is actual and exhaustive. The archived class definition starts from either K4 or K5 and permits attachment along any existing edge, including repeated choices. Its defect-one classification therefore applies to the constructed support.

For J1=bd, I checked the three-page witnesses and every possible same-old-clique footprint pair. For acx,dey,abd only bd and dy can share a clique; for bcx,aey,abd only bd and bx can do so. In both cases this pair is adjacent, so an old maximum packing avoids the complete footprint. Outside apices may coincide without creating edge collisions. Single-edge resilience rules out a book on ab or ad, including the singleton-central-triangle ambiguity. Consequently a K4 family gives an actual m+3 packing and both families must be books.

In the both-book case, supported bd would allow an old minimum cover through bd plus the two book bases, saving one. Thus bd is background. Any disjoint pair of ordinary pages appends together with abd: per old component the two page footprints, if both present, form an adjacent star at a; every other core-terminal pair is absent or is background bd. Hence every cross-family ordinary pair intersects. Since apices are outside the core, the only possibility is the adjacent bases ac,ae with the same apex for every page. This forces exactly the five W5 triangles. Each wheel edge belongs to a minimum three-edge wheel cover, so a supported old wheel edge would again save one by overlap. All old wheel edges are background, and connected triangle support removes every separate old B component. The support is exactly W5.

The J0 fresh C12 supports and the J1 W5 support are admissible at the modular interface. Their exclusion in the actual-parent application is a separate invocation of the archived strict triangle-restoration results with arbitrary background. No two-edge resilience or minimum-Q boundary theorem is imported into H. The J2 cases remain separately reviewed.
