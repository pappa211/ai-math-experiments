# Independent canonical outside-packing review

Status: ACCEPTED ORDINARY at the specified compatible-page interface. No computation or Lean check. Bound source: notes/matching26_canonical_outside_packing_v1.md SHA-256 337743ea3a7cf525f8d95f195afba3c913f3af7a9a5dfa58a473034db6ca7dbe.

The canonical choice maximizing the outside-triangle count among maximum L packings is finite and valid. A triangle in a usable endpoint link is an actual L triangle avoiding both endpoints. It misses the retained outside and opposite-endpoint packing. Its conflicts with the same-endpoint fan are only rim edges, of which that fan's matching uses at most one. Zero conflicts augments; one permits a size-preserving replacement increasing the outside count. Thus both usable endpoint links are triangle-free for arbitrary codegree. This is not a bipartiteness claim.

At codegree exactly two, an actual outside triangle missing M_0 cannot meet both endpoint rim matchings: their two intersecting rim edges would share a common neighbor, but old L endpoint fans avoid both deleted page apices. It meets at most one fan triangle, contradicting either maximum size or the canonical choice. Thus E(M_0) is indeed a transversal of every actual outside triangle, without naming or deleting the outside graph. An unpacked triangle shares at most one edge with each packed label.

If both usable endpoint links are empty, their contained old fan matchings are empty. W=Q-{u,v} therefore has packing number m=k-1. For any endpoint rim r other than xy, choose one of the two reserved apices outside its endpoints. A maximum W packing avoiding r would accept the endpoint triangle and the disjoint reserved uv-page, giving k+1. Hence r is critical in W, with an exact drop of one. This argument concerns every maximum W packing, not just the canonical one. In fact this particular critical-rim argument needs only the two reserved apices and the empty-fan compatible interface, not the absence of further common neighbors; no larger-codegree third-hub or compression conclusion follows from that observation.

The restriction of a minimum K cover through P_x to W removes at least two cover edges, proving only tau(W)<=2m-2. The source correctly supplies no lower bound, no resilience, no connectedness, and no fixed-G naming of W. The all-maximum critical-rim constraint is consistent with the separately accepted prohibition on using a mixed hub triangle to occupy differently marked rims together.

The elementary triangle-free cover estimate is valid. In a maximum matching, both endpoints of a matched edge cannot have distinct unmatched neighbors, because those make a length-three augmenting path; a shared unmatched neighbor would form a triangle. Hence one endpoint has no unmatched neighbor and can be removed from the usual matched-vertex cover. This gives a vertex cover of size at most 2r-1 when the graph is nonempty. The empty case must use cover zero separately. Applying it to the h-deleted usable links is legitimate; no equality between matching and vertex-cover numbers is asserted. The remaining cost of covering outside triangles is not paid by this estimate alone.

## Arbitrary-codegree extension of the canonical obstruction

The same proof gives the coordinator's proposed exact geometry without any new assumption. If an actual triangle T avoiding u,v misses E(M_0), it must meet exactly one M_u rim and exactly one M_v rim, since zero or one conflict contradicts canonicality. These two distinct triangle edges share a vertex z in N_Q(u) intersection N_Q(v) other than the two deleted page apices. Write them za and zb; the actual outside triangle is zab. The third edge ab is unused by the entire packing M: it misses M_0 by assumption and cannot be another fan-matching edge because it meets the already selected rim in each fan. Thus the old packed triangles are uza and vzb, and ab is an actual unoccupied closing edge.

This is a checked obstruction geometry, not yet an augmentation. Replacing those two endpoint triangles by zab and uvz preserves the number of triangles in Q but supplies no extra unit, and uvz cannot coexist with another uv-page. A further paid packing or cover operation is required. In particular the codegree-two release-compression proof cannot simply be reused when these additional common apices remain.
