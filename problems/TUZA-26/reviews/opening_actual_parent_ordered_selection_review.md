# Independent ordered actual-parent triangle selection review

Status: ACCEPTED ORDINARY, conditional on the stated all-double-defect-two branch. This proves two actual nested selections, not selection of a failing or tight-residual triple. No numerical or Lean verification is claimed.

The first application of the maximal-support lemma uses the exact single-triangle cover decrement in G. For the fixed resulting H=G-T, every actual triangle U is edge-disjoint from T, so the all-double-defect-two equation gives tau(H-U)=tau(H)-3. Thus U is wholly contained in a minimum H cover, and the same lemma legitimately applies a second time. The graph for this second maximization is fixed H, and no tightness, minimum-Q property or defect-two resilience is invoked. The supplied single-residual profile ensures H has triangles. There is no justified third application, because R's triangle-cover decrement is not known to be three.

For the conditional common-center tight cube, an old bare de-page has spokes dp,ep background in B. The selected U makes at least one supported in R=B+V. Such new support must use a restored V edge. If the page apex is outside V's terminals, a spoke and a restored V edge have four distinct endpoints and cannot belong to one triangle. The common-center apex a is forbidden by the missing old ad,ae edges. Therefore the only possible page apices are f,g; this also excludes b,c from the first deleted triangle.

For apex f, the only possible new triangles through df use either fg with old dg or af with old ad. The latter old edge is absent. Likewise ef can be activated only by old eg. Hence the two existing page spokes df,ef require at least one of dg,eg. The symmetric calculation holds for apex g. This exactly gives the stated three-or-four-edge cross interface, with every actual restored-edge possibility accounted for. It is a necessary activation condition, not proof that the interface exists or can be paid.

The first selected T restores U and V together in its residual, so only the weaker exclusion of outside-seven-core bare apices follows there. Neither the second selection's stronger confinement nor bare-freeness of all three families is transferred to it. A suitable low-packing tight third residual containing the chosen pair remains unavailable, and the other triple profiles remain open.
