# Independent shared exceptional-family packing review

Status: ACCEPTED ORDINARY at the stated complete-family interface. This is an actual packing augmentation and makes no mixed-triangle cover assumption. No computation or Lean check is claimed.

All full apices lie outside the five restored terminals, because a required spoke to their shared terminal would otherwise be one of the other triangle's missing bases. Within either full-apex pair, an old edge would join two old triangles and force a missing restored base into one literal old clique. Thus the pair is nonadjacent.

The four pages abx1,bcx2,ady1,dey2 are pairwise edge-disjoint after choosing x1!=y1. Between families only an a-apex spoke could coincide: reciprocal terminal/apex identifications are impossible because every apex is outside the entire core. Equalities among other outside apices cause only vertex sharing.

Each old clique sees at most one spoke per exceptional family, so at most two footprint edges. If these are disjoint and one uses terminal a, the clique contains a forbidden restored base. Otherwise they are b'x and d'y. The clique supplies old b'y and xy; the U full-apex spoke ay then creates an ordinary T page ab'y. Completeness forces y to be a T full apex. As x and y are distinct, xy contradicts the nonadjacency of that apex pair. Thus every two-edge footprint is adjacent. The exhibited K4/K5 maximum packings avoid it; all old components choose independently even with shared vertices. The four pages append over a full old maximum, proving nu>=m+4. Every retained old edge collision has been accounted for; extra mixed triangles do not impede this packing.

The stated common-center cube corollary follows. A direct additional corollary also follows at the already reviewed surviving low-B cube scope: the three restored triangles are pairwise vertex-intersecting in BOTH the common-center and cyclic intersection patterns. Applying this lemma to every pair shows at most one exceptional family in either pattern. The other families therefore have individual two-edge covers. This does not prove that their union hits any mixed triangle; the earlier invalid cover-sum shortcut remains quarantined, and a complete mixed-triangle payment is still required.
