# Independent release-set compression review

Status: ACCEPTED ORDINARY at the exact codegree-two endpoint-link interface, with fixed maximum L packing and one fixed reserved hub. No computation or Lean verification. The proof does not assume submodularity of matching rank in triangle labels.

After deleting the same hub from both usable links, their common vertex set has at most the other common neighbor g. Consequently their selected physical rim edges are disjoint. A released packed triangle supplies at most one selected edge to each endpoint matching. If it supplies both, the two distinct selected edges meet at g. Since each endpoint matching can use g only once, at most one released triangle can be used by both. Thus the total number n of selected newly released rims is at most |S|+1.

Deleting all newly released rims gives matchings in the original hub-deleted links. Their sizes are bounded by m_u,m_v, so a_u+a_v<=n. A profitable set forces equality in both inequalities. The deficits of the two old remainders are individually nonnegative, so equality of their sum forces EACH old remainder to have its full original size. Equality also forces one doubly used label and every other released label to be used once. Adding back only the pivotal triangle's selected rim to each old remainder gives both singleton gains. A singleton gain is at most one because no matching can select two edges of one triangle. Hence both gains equal one exactly.

The neutral-repacking description is also sound, not merely a rank argument. The pivotal rims meet at g, so both old remainders avoid g as well as the deleted hub h. Their endpoint fans are therefore actual L triangles. They avoid all M_0 rim edges and cannot collide with each other's spokes or rims: opposite endpoints are excluded from the links, and the two non-hub link vertex sets are disjoint under codegree two. Thus they replace the original endpoint families while retaining the SAME outside packing M_0 and the same maximum packing size. Their exclusive vertices a,b are unmatched after the pivotal rims are removed, so ua,vb are unused. Replacing gab by uga,vgb,uvh is an actual three-for-one Q augmentation.

The exact limitation follows by contraposition. In this fixed-M_0, same-reserved-hub, two-endpoint-fan framework, no jointly releasing singleton means no profitable release set of ANY size. This rules out a strategy of merely increasing the number of released packed labels; it does not rule out replacing the outside packing with other actual L triangles, changing the interface, or another kind of augmentation. It also does not prove the singleton is available. The codegree-two assumption is essential to the unique common vertex after deleting h; the codegree-three star control is correctly excluded from this theorem's scope.

Strategic consequence: a genuine new bridge must either force the pivotal occupied triangle (possibly after a justified neutral change of the maximum outside packing), or provide an actual outside-packing replacement that creates it. Abstract closest-cover reachability alone still does not distinguish occupied rims from rims belonging to different packed triangles. This is a structural obstruction to broadening the current release-set search, not a counterexample satisfying the full minimum-Q profile.
