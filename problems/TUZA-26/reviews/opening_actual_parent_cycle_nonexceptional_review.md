These exclusions force p,q,r distinct and keep every extra ax-apex s distinct from them. The only possible core value of s is y; no freshness assumption is needed.

Potential old-spoke collisions with xp or ar would require s=p or s=r, already excluded; full apices are outside the core. The permitted s=y creates axy and causes no edge collision.

After removing known background bp,cq,ar, the complete supported footprint is contained in xp,yq,zr,as,xs. Two full-apex spokes force one of the forbidden off-diagonal incidences. Coexistence of xs with yq or zr forces xq or xr, while coexistence with xp is excluded by the whole ordinary ax-family footprint fact. Finally as and xs force missing ax. Thus every old component sees at most one distinct supported footprint edge, including when s=y. A full old packing avoids these edges and appends all five triangles, contradicting gain four.

All old components, arbitrary shared vertices, background and mixed triangles are accounted for in these actual cover or packing payments. The conclusion is precisely that a surviving cyclic cube must have an exceptional family; it is not a global triple-selection or all-cube theorem.
