# An unconditional selected-triangle dichotomy in the actual parent

Frozen ordinary corollary v1, TUZA-26, 2026-11-17. Independent review requested. This strengthens the quantifier scope of the ordered selection in apex_actual_parent_ordered_selection_v1.md; it does not settle either resulting branch.

Let G be the fixed minimum-edge CS failure, nu(G)=k,tau(G)=2k, with the accepted actual single-residual profile, simultaneous extension of two triangles, and no tight double residual. Choose an actual triangle T maximizing the number of supported edges of H=G-E(T), over ALL actual triangles of G.

Every triangle of G is wholly contained in some minimum G cover because tau(G-T)=tau(G)-3. The reviewed maximal-support selection lemma therefore proves that no ordinary T-page has both spokes background in H. This first choice is always available; it has no all-double-defect-two assumption.

Now exactly one of the following alternatives holds.

**I. A defect-one partner exists for this selected T.** There is an actual triangle U of H such that R=H-E(U) has defect one. The independently reviewed actual-parent theorems give nu(R)=k-2, tau(R)=2k-5, single-edge packing resilience, and extension of every actual R-triangle to a maximum R-packing. R is not asserted triangle-connected or named. No no-bare-page conclusion for U is inferred, since U need not lie wholly in a minimum H cover.

**II. Every partner of this selected T has defect two.** For every actual triangle U of THIS fixed H, tau(H-U)=2k-6=tau(H)-3. Hence every H-triangle is wholly contained in some minimum H cover. Choose U maximizing the supported-edge count of R=H-U over all actual H-triangles. Applying the same general selection lemma to H proves that no ordinary U-page has both spokes background in R. Its parameters are nu(R)=k-2,tau(R)=2k-6; its defect-two support is not named and resilience is not asserted.

The dichotomy is exhaustive because every actual double residual has defect one or two. The second branch requires only the defect-two responses through the selected T, not that EVERY pair of triangles in G has defect two. The first selection is global over actual G triangles, so the bare-page exchange cannot escape its extremal domain. The second selection is over the fixed H, so its exchange likewise stays in its legitimate domain.

The H-triangle selection is nonempty at the actual-parent scope: H has connected nonempty defect-one support. No replacement graph outside G or minimum-Q premise is used.
