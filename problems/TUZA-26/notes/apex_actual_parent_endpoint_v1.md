## 1. Main implication boundary

The coordinator's actual-parent composition proves that every defect-one double-triangle residual of the literal minimum-CS parent is single-edge resilient and every one of its triangles extends to a maximum packing. This does not prove that a defect-one double residual exists.

The maximal-support triangle rule is a legitimate selection operation. Choose T maximizing supported edges of H=G-T. Then no restored ordinary T-page is bare relative to H. The coordinator's final refinement, actual_parent_selected_triangle_dichotomy_v2.md, chooses U maximizing supported edges of R=H-U unconditionally. If it has defect two, tau(R)=tau(H)-3 makes this selected U fully minimum-cover eligible, so no ordinary U-page is bare relative to R. Neither branch is contradicted. A third no-bare conclusion is not justified without the selected third triangle's full-cover eligibility.

The original ordered-selection source's all-pairs-defect-two premise remains sufficient but is unnecessary for this refined dichotomy. No minimum-Q properties transfer to H or R. ## 4. Accepted common-center endpoint

Write the restored triangles abc,ade,afg. The exact ordinary-family matching normal form has three types:

* A: two active bases with disjoint apex sets;
* B: one full apex plus additional pages on one base;
* C: the exceptional two-full-apex K5-e family.

These are page-family forms, not additions to the frozen C12 graph family.

The following combinations/regions are paid by ordinary cover or packing arguments with complete old footprints and all mixed triangles retained:

* Three A families: their six own-base cover edges leave mixed rims on three omitted-spoke leaves. An old minimum cover makes that rim graph triangle-free, so one further hub spoke gives a cover of size at most2m+7.
* Three B families: two hub-base extra families are excluded by the shared-pair operation. Three opposite-base extra families are paid for all full-apex equality patterns. Exactly one hub-base extra family is paid by the separate one-hub theorem, including all apex identifications. Therefore all-B common-center cubes are excluded.
* A complete K4 conflict star with supported spokes bp,dp,fp and background partners cp,ep,gp: the entire mixed rim graph is triangle bdf; the chosen old cover hits one rim, and one hub spoke completes a saving.
* Two bare opposite pages with outside-core apices, plus a core-leaf perfect matching avoiding their bases: five actual triangles append. Every old component sees at most one matching rim. This operation is conditional on its literal supplied pages and matching.

At most one C family remains. Consequently the common-center cases not paid here are mixtures AAB or ABB, and cases with one C and two other families. The normal-form cover and maximal-support activation constraints are retained, but they have not supplied a complete cover saving or packing augmentation in all those cases. No additional family pattern is being researched at this endpoint.

## 5. Exact remaining activation obstruction

For the ordered selected pair U=ade inside H, if a low-B common-center cube exists with remaining V=afg, then R=B+V. Every B-bare ordinary opposite U-page must have apex f or g. For a def page, at least one of dg,eg must be present to activate df or ef through fg; the symmetric statement holds at g.

This is a genuine available selection constraint conditional on the supplied cube. It does not say that the page is absent: a B-background spoke can become supported after restoring V. Likewise a B-bare hub page adp can have ap activated by a V-page afp or agp. Thus no-R-bare cannot be replaced by no-B-bare. Nonbare pages may still meet arbitrary old components.

The cover discriminator forces two disjoint uncovered mixed rims after an appropriate old minimum cover and six additional individual-family cover edges. Their rim footprint is avoidable, but three further compatible pages have not been forced. This is the exact retained packing/cover gap, not a claim that a surviving configuration exists.
