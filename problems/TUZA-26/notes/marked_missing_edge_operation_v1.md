## First two-page interface

When X={x,y}, choose minimum K covers F_x,F_y containing P_x,P_y respectively. F_x must omit BOTH P_y edges: otherwise it already meets both new pages and covers Q too cheaply. The reverse holds for F_y.

If these covers agree outside the four spokes, their common outside part plus {ux,vy} is a cover of K and of both new pages at the same cost. Indeed any triangle not hit by the common part must meet both P_x and P_y; the only actual possibilities are uxy and vxy. This contradicts the target. Therefore an outside cover exchange is necessary.

Every cross pair choosing one edge of P_x and one of P_y is a NONblocker in K: deleting it kills both uv-pages, so Q and K have identical surviving triangle families, and Q's two-edge resilience gives packing k. Meanwhile each P_x/P_y blocks. Also L=K-P_x-P_y has packing k-1: a maximum packing of Q-P_x must use the sole surviving uv-page uvy; removing it gives a size k-1 packing avoiding all four spokes. Its cover number lies in {2k-5,2k-4}. The upper bound restricts F_x; the lower follows by extending an L cover with all four spokes to cover Q. Thus deleting all four spokes is NOT yet paid: it loses one packing unit and can need three or four cover edges to restore.

## Exact cover-exchange projection, and its unresolved triangle obstruction

For two minimum K covers, write D=F_x intersect F_y, A=F_x minus D, B=F_y minus D. Then |A|=|B|. Every actual triangle unhit by D meets both A and B. Choose one A/B pair from each such triangle and form a bipartite graph H on A,B with these pairs as edges. Any ordinary vertex cover of H, together with D, covers K. Minimum-cover optimality and the elementary bipartite matching-cover theorem therefore force H to have a perfect matching, for EVERY such choice of pairs.

This is a valid necessary projection. Its matching is NOT automatically a triangle packing: the corresponding actual triangles can share their third graph edges, including other A/B edges. A usable exchange must either produce a minimum H cover that hits all missing-edge pages, or control those actual third-edge conflicts in a packing augmentation. That is the current narrower unresolved bridge, not a replacement definition of Tuza.
