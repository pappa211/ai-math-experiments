# Failed approaches and limits retained

1. **Natural dense-neighborhood deletion alone does not force equality.** For K6 the remainder has deficit1or2; for K6-e it has deficit1. Invoking clique blocks at positive slack would be invalid. The free essential-edge page mechanism proves stability in deficit1, and localization isolates a defect, but these observations alone did not close the primary problem. The completed proof instead uses pair overlaps and global degeneracy.
2. **False three-spoke avoidance.** A K5 maximum packing can avoid anytwoedges, not an arbitrary3edge star. The essential-edge argument required an actually free page, not simply adding a third forbidden spoke. The final proof uses at mosttwo forbiddenedges perK5block, and onlystar footprints in K4.
3. **Withdrawn degree7 arithmetic.** A provisional nondominating-neighbor cover was counted as10edges although6+1+3+1=11. No accepted inference uses it. The successful global proof avoids that branch altogether.
4. **Optional detours.** P7/P8 maximumdegree corollaries and the generalized stability-loss note remain research notes; they are unnecessary for the main theorem and were excluded from its final fresh-review scope.
5. **No finite search shortcut.** The45rim-pair check concerns one fixed graph only. Arbitrary attachments require the page argument. No absolute finite search reduction, fractional stability input, or union of individual cover-preserving deletions is asserted.
6. **No automatic wider consequences.** Five-degeneracy does not follow from an OCT-five set or from a tripartite full triangle hypergraph. The theorem does not settle unrestricted restoration, general Tuza, or global equality. The broad independent-set deletion conjecture from the secondary route was not pursued or proved.
7. **Prior-art scope.** Maximum degree, maximum average degree and degeneracy cannot be interchanged. The supplied Zenodo paper concerns packingnumber4, not this target. No claim of historical priority follows from a negative search.
