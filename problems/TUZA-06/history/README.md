# TUZA-04 and TUZA-05 research history

These are curated public derivatives of frozen research records. They preserve the ordinary mathematical arguments, retained relevant failed approaches and stage-specific limitations; operational clocks, packaging, machine identifiers, full third-party articles and build objects are omitted. Nothing here retroactively changes the original frozen checkpoints.

- TUZA-04: [boundary route](tuza04/BOUNDARY_ROUTE.md), [equality route](tuza04/EQUALITY_ROUTE.md), [equality theorems and ordinary proofs](tuza04/EQUALITY_THEOREMS.md), [independent equality review](tuza04/REVIEW_EQUALITY_FRESH.md), [literature positioning](tuza04/LITERATURE_ROUTE.md).
- TUZA-05: [complete ordinary theorem/proof manuscript](tuza05/THEOREMS_AND_PROOFS.md), [review](tuza05/REVIEW.md), [prior-art account](tuza05/PRIOR_ART.md), [failed approaches](tuza05/FAILED_APPROACHES.md), [retained mathematical route audits](tuza05/notes/).

TUZA-04 developed triangle-component and boundary structure. TUZA-05 supplied the combined five-degenerate ordinary proof. TUZA-06 consolidated attribution and the ordinary proof, completed the full Lean development and verified the exact unconditional combined theorem. Historical partial or conditional statuses describe their own stage; the [final status](../PROOF_STATUS.md) controls the published claim. Earlier operational reproduction commands are retired; use the [portable TUZA-06 entry points](../REPRODUCTION.md).
