## Scope and priority qualifications

Puleo's main theorem is for maximum average degree below seven. No implication from 5-degeneracy to that hypothesis is available. Botler–Fernandes–Gutierrez prove a treewidth theorem; their local lemma, rather than an invented degeneracy version of their main theorem, is reused. Neither source is claimed to have already proved the full combined theorem of this manuscript.

The equality classification and its attachment compatibility are the leading proposed contribution. A short inequality deduction can also be a new deduction; a short proof and negative searches do not determine priority. No exhaustive historical-priority investigation was attempted. No human mathematical authorship, specialist endorsement, or completed Gemini review is invented. The research/proofs were developed through AI systems, while the human participant supplied objectives, budgets, review coordination, and authorization.


## Distinguishing the two four/eight reductions

Puleo's Proposition 5.1, proof Case 1, treats the singleton $K_{2,2,1}$ link: when the missing link edges are $ab,cd$, the packing is $vbd,vac,ade,bce$ and all eight link edges form the cover. The retained TUZA-04 K221 route refers to this witness. Proposition 5.2, proof Case 1, instead treats adjacent degree-five vertices and gives a different four-triangle/eight-edge reducing pair; this is the antecedent credited in canonical paper Section 6.2. The identical numerical sizes do not identify the two constructions. Both require the additional combined-equality compatibility arguments supplied by the current proof.
