# Targeted novelty and version check before publication

Checked on **28 October 2026 UTC**, during final manuscript preparation. This was a short update to Validation V1's source-based review, not an exhaustive new literature campaign. **No exact prior equivalent or established subsuming theorem was located. Originality remains unconfirmed.** No specialist correspondence or external review occurred.

Direct primary-record checks:

| Record | Latest version observed | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| [Liu–Zeng, 2610.15539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15539) | v1, 11 October 2026 | The abstract still states the fixed range $c<2$. The archived full v1 proof supplies the three cases adapted in the manuscript; it does not itself state our $c<5/2$ conclusion. |
| [Haxell–Narins–Szabó, 1401.0171](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0171) | v3, 20 June 2016 | Earlier home-base structural preprint. The manuscript uses the [2018 published paper's author manuscript](https://page.mi.fu-berlin.de/szabo/PDF/ryser.pdf) for theorem/definition numbering. |
| [Wang, 2609.13831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13831) | v1, 12 September 2026 | A universal factor above two, not an exact factor-two result subsuming these classes. Its computational/formalization claims were not reproduced here. |
| [Clow–Haxell–Mohar, 2505.05339](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05339) | v2, 10 June 2025 | Counterexample to a general matching-reducing deletion assertion for tripartite hypergraphs; it does not contradict the HNS equality case. |

Search formulations included:

- Tuza, triangle deletion/addition, triangle-3-colorable remainders, and Haxell–Narins–Szabó.
- Tuza with odd-cycle transversal four, OCT four, bipartization by four vertices, and deletion of four vertices to bipartite.
- Tuza density with fixed linear deficit, Liu–Zeng, and $5/2$.
- Home-base/Ryser equality with graph triangles, graphical $K_4$ structure, and Tuza applications.

The targeted searches produced existing coloring/equality literature and unrelated parameterized-deletion results, but no new primary theorem covering the full claims. Search snippets or absence of a hit were not treated as a mathematical proof of novelty. The [historical comparison](../research-notes/NOVELTY_REVIEW.md) supplies the already checked primary-source distinctions: planar equality requires planarity; fractional equality requires a fractional hypothesis; bounded-degree, bounded-packing, split, and ordinary multipartite cases do not contain the full deletion/OCT class.

The accessible [Yuster publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/rsa.20048) and [Haxell–Kostochka–Thomassé publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0195669811001727) were checked for bibliographic precision. Two initial bibliography DOI typos were corrected before publication. The primary mathematical critic independently checked HNS's formal home-base definitions, the graph conversion, and the Liu–Zeng cases against the source text.

Limits remain: the original Aharoni theorem number and LBT's triangle-3-colorable theorem number were not verified from accessible original full text; they are not guessed. Unindexed/inaccessible literature, alternative terminology and specialist knowledge may contain prior art. The manuscript and project README therefore retain unrefereed status and unconfirmed novelty prominently.
