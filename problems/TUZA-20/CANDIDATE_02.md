# Candidate 02: all gains extend within the global spoke budget

Status: CONJECTURE. Revision motivated by the certified six-vertex C01 failure, not a change to PK-all's quantifiers.

For every finite simple graph G with a specified maximum all-type-one triangle packing P of size k, select all private bases R. For every singleton owner a_i b_i x_i with unique private page a_i b_i z_i whose gain x_i z_i is an actual edge, collect the distinct gains outside R into F. These edges are original packed spokes. Claim: there exists a spoke set S containing F, meeting EVERY actual residual two- or three-literal clause, with |S|<=k.

Unlike C01, all remaining k-|F| units may be used on any spokes, including those of lenders, with zero/one/two allocations allowed. A repeated gain or a gain already in R releases budget globally. Exceptional K5 pairs receive no separate restrictive choice model. All actual clauses are retained; no Hall condition, packing rotation, bounded-radius progress or termination assertion is assumed.

If true, this removes the global type-one existence obligation and gives the explicit fixed support cover R union S of size <=2k. Mixed type-zero and private-K4 compatibility remains open. The proposed graph-specific ingredient is gain-edge ownership coupled to singleton-page geometry and maximum-cardinality augmentations, but no proof is established. Mandatory gains may still obstruct an otherwise affordable cover; this stronger restriction is the precise target of falsification.

Decisive test: minimize the number of additional spokes after fixing R union F, using all actual triangles. A counterexample must certify maximum P, minimum constrained cover >2k, and unrestricted c_G(P) separately. It refutes forced-gain extension, not PK-all unless c_G(P)>2k also holds.
