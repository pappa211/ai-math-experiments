# C21-D screen: extend a maximum private-witness packing

Recorded before testing. Status CONJECTURE, exploratory serious screen after the private-only C21-C failure. This uses a minimum ordinary cover, not PK allowed support.

1. **Exact statement D-all.** For every finite simple G and every minimum triangle-edge cover C, define F_C={T actual triangle: |E(T) intersect C|=1}. For every maximum edge-disjoint packing Q of F_C, every inclusion-maximal packing P of the full triangle family satisfying Q subset P has |C|<=2|P|. Q is maximum in F_C, while P is only inclusion-maximal in all triangles. We separately distinguish D-some-extension: for every such C,Q there exists a full packing extension P with the bound; and D-some-choice: some minimum C and some maximum Q have such an extension. Failure of D-all alone refutes neither weaker statement.

2. **Unrestricted implication and obligations.** A minimum C and maximum Q exist; greedily extend Q to a maximal full P. D-all would give tau=|C|<=2|P|<=2nu. No packing-support restriction, type taxonomy or completion accounting is needed. The entire new obligation is to prove the extension inequality, including full maximality and exact private optimality. This logical implication does not prove the hypothesis.

3. **Graph-specific reason.** For a cone over a triangle-free link L, full triangle packings are ordinary matchings in L. Every maximal matching has a vertex-cover endpoint set, hence 2|P|>=tau(cone L). This specifically repairs the private-only counterexample, whose F_C omits link edges inside the spoke-cover vertex set. More generally, actual triangles with two or three C edges may pay for witness conflicts.

4. **Two merits, two objections.** Merits: all C-edge color patterns are retained; and maximum private-witness Q supplies a concrete extra constraint missing from arbitrary maximal triangle packings (the three-sun shows arbitrary maximality fails). Objections: committing to Q can lock out efficient full packings, unlike matchings; and no graph-specific two-capacity charging or augmentation lemma is yet known, so survival alone is not a mechanism. Minimum cover optimality does not imply interchangeable private witnesses.

5. **Cheapest falsification.** Find exact minimum C, exact maximum F_C-packing Q and an inclusion-maximal full extension P with |C|>2|P|. An especially simple witness has Q=P. Reconstruct every actual triangle. Target fixed controls, merged K4/K5 cells, and local templates around a red triangle; no generic graph census. Cap whole run at90s under external2200MiB process-tree monitor; per-case bounds and capped cover/packing enumeration retained as UNKNOWN unexamined states. A matching explicit packing plus exact optimum calculation certifies maximum Q. D-some-extension additionally needs exact maximum extension below threshold, not one bad greedy P.

6. **First nontrivial lemma.** Try to show that a full maximal packing containing an F_C-maximum subpacking can charge the edges of C to its triangles with capacity2, using minimum-cover exchange and literal endpoint geometry. A useful local start would show that a purported three-charge obstruction either frees an actual full triangle or improves Q in F_C. This proposed start is not yet a proved availability theorem; a mere restatement of D-all or an unconstrained assignment will not count as progress.

7. **Disposition rules.** Retire D-all on an exact witness; consider a weaker quantifier only if the diagnosed obstruction suggests a new concrete exchange mechanism. If targeted controls survive but no realizable local obstruction lemma can be formulated, record STALLED, not global advance. Do not expand a survival census or assert that cone matching behavior transfers to general triangle packing.
