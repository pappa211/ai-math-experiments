# Regeneration assessment after the initial routes stalled

Recommendation: **do not promote a surviving reformulation into the main effort**. The campaign has considered a materially different minimum-ordinary-cover architecture, including the refuted private-only proposal C21-C and a full-triangle extension successor C21-D. The remaining proposals below have explicit gaps and no concrete next structural action justified by this scout. This is not a claim that Tuza, H-D or PK is false or that research on them should end permanently.

## Why minimum covers constitute a different architecture

The initial PK program fixes a maximum triangle packing and restricts cover choices to its support/completions. The regenerated program fixes a minimum ordinary cover C and studies private witness triangles with exactly one C-edge. It has neither a permitted-support restriction nor the old compulsory-gain rule. The Clebsch-cone obstruction communicated by the coordinator defeats the private-only universal packing bound: tau is n−alpha, while a private packing uses distinct vertices of a maximum independent set and has at most alpha triangles. This analysis does not depend on the scout's failed lender-only pair reduction.

The cause is loss of the triangles with two or three C edges. Their presence cannot be inferred from private witnesses alone, but they can be essential to the full packing. The scout suggested checking whether all Clebsch-cone minimum covers are forced to be spoke covers; the separate verifier owns the final answer and certificate for that strengthening. No unchecked claim about all minimum covers is imported here.

## C21-D: maximum private packing followed by full extension

The seven-item pretest admission record is `notes/pk_successor_candidate.md`. Its strongest version says: every maximum packing Q of F_C extends, by any inclusion-maximal full extension P, to |C|<=2|P|. The weaker quantifiers “every Q has some successful extension” and “some C,Q has a successful extension” are explicitly separate. A bad greedy extension would not refute the existential versions.

There is a genuine reason this repairs the cone obstruction. Let G be the cone over a triangle-free graph L. Every actual triangle is apex plus a link edge, and edge-disjoint triangles correspond exactly to matchings in L. Further tau(G)=vertex-cover-number(L): selecting apex spokes at a link vertex cover gives the upper bound. Conversely, replace each selected rim edge of any triangle cover by one incident apex spoke. This never increases cost and covers the unique triangle using that rim edge (the link is triangle-free), giving a spoke cover and hence a vertex cover of L. For every maximal matching M, its endpoint set covers every link edge, because an edge with both endpoints unmatched could be appended. Thus tau(G)<=2|M| for every maximal full triangle packing, regardless of the initial Q. This is an elementary known matching mechanism, not a new general triangle theorem.

The attempted generalization would need to replace that endpoint-cover fact by a graph-specific charge or exchange theorem: full maximality plus maximum cardinality of the private subpacking must rule out more than two minimum-cover edges charged to one packed triangle. The scout found no such theorem. Outside a cone, disjointness can fail through each of three triangle edges and through different endpoints; the matching proof does not survive merely by calling those interactions augmenting paths. Arbitrary maximal packings already fail the desired bound on the three-sun, so the private optimality must do real additional work.

## Other materially different representations considered

**All C-edge color patterns.** Retain every triangle classified by whether it contains one, two or three C edges, rather than throwing away the latter two classes. Minimum-cover optimality says that for every X subset C, replacing X requires a cover of all triangles whose C-intersection is contained in X. This is exact and exposes the information lost by F_C, but it is still a complete constraint system. No laminarity, matching duality, exchange axiom or integrality statement was derived from graph endpoints. Claiming that compatible color states exist would merely restate the unrestricted obligation.

**Triangle-free extension from the opposite side.** A minimum cover C is the complement of a maximum-cardinality triangle-free spanning subgraph H. One could try to use a failed enlargement of H to construct a sufficiently large triangle packing, allowing edge deletions and insertions globally. This changes the optimization object and avoids fixed packing-support choices. But maximality of H alone gives only private triangle witnesses, already insufficient; maximum cardinality gives a global replacement inequality without a usable bounded exchange or potential. No argument identifies a universally available augmentation or proves termination with the factor2 budget. Restricting H to a bipartite or specially decomposable subgraph would add an unavailable structural hypothesis.

**Multiple-apex matching interfaces.** The cone proof suggests coupling several link matchings. With two apices, link edges can be shared between their triangle families, so matching choices acquire additional conflicts; with more apices, mixed triangles may also appear. An exact state optimizer can preserve these constraints but does not prove compatibility. A new subclass theorem without a reduction from general graphs would not discharge an unrestricted obligation. No such reduction or load-bearing interface was available, so this was not promoted into a parameter-expansion project.

## Campaign decision relevance

The regeneration stage therefore did more than rename optional gains: it reversed the packing/cover starting point, tested a precise private-witness assertion, diagnosed its failure through lost color patterns, and screened a full-triangle extension successor. The surviving representations require a new structural lemma before they justify substantial further effort. On the evidence available to this scout, there is no concrete supported next step beyond that missing theorem. An early closeout after independent verification of the actual counterexample is defensible under the mission's stopping rule; it retires current mechanisms and records open alternatives, without declaring a wider impossibility or authorizing a follow-on campaign.

No historical-originality claim, new Lean proof, external human review, remote computation, public write or outreach is asserted.
