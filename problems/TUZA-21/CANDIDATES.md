## Exploratory ideas not promoted

The lender/K5-only residual pair kernel fails on the wheel because it omits the published common-tip fibre case. This is a screening error, not a new serious global theorem or a PK refutation. A proposed separated-attachment blocker-rigidity statement would require an unavailable unrestricted equality structure; it was not granted an assumed lemma. Color-pattern state systems, triangle-free complement exchange, and multiple-apex matching were considered in regeneration but lacked a concrete compatibility/augmentation theorem, so they were not presented as proved or as promising merely for being exact formulations.

## Why the all-size gap remains

A1 offers an actual residual-K4 geometry mechanism at k2; it does not supply a K4 residual cell for arbitrary k or an outside packing avoiding two attachment edges. C21-C had a concrete representation change and was exactly falsified. D has a concrete matching repair only in cones; its noncone screen had zero private-deficient cases and therefore no evidentiary leverage for the new difficulty. Further work needs a genuinely new structural step rather than a larger passing census. See REGENERATION.md for the campaign decision.
