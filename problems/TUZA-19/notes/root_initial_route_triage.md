## Binary-matroid reformulation
Nomoto and van der Pol, *Tuza’s Conjecture for Binary Geometries*, SIAM J. Discrete Math.38(2)(2024),1676–1685, DOI10.1137/22M1511229, https://ris.utwente.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/421363158/22m1511229.pdf . The inspected introduction states their conjecture for simple binary matroids without an F7 restriction and proves the cographic case. Graphic matroids retain exactly the original triangle parameters. Rephrasing Tuza in this larger class supplies no new availability or rounding step. The paper’s cographic proof uses small bonds and a crown forced by cut structure; general graph triangles do not supply that separation. No published theorem here is being used to conclude unrestricted Tuza.

## Directed-triangle transfer
McDonald, Puleo and Tennenhouse, *Packing and covering directed triangles*, arXiv:1806.08809v2, https://arxiv.org/html/1806.08809 . The proof uses a bipartite source-sink network for triangles incident to a vertex, and adds the packed base arcs to a Menger cover. Undirected links have a matching/vertex-cover gap, so this step does not transfer verbatim. Bidirected K4 already allows four pairwise arc-disjoint directed faces (the oriented boundary of a tetrahedron); ordinary ν(K4)=1. Thus replacing every edge by opposite arcs does not preserve packing even with factor2. No nontrivial global orientation-transfer hypothesis survived this implication check; no census launched.

## Finite-incidence prior-art overlap
Guruswami and Sandeep, ECCC Report2020/167 Theorem3, https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2020/167/download/ , gives the near-rank matching/cover gap after excluding finitely many configurations absent from pair blow-ups (ordinary non-induced containment). TUZA-18 explicitly treats induced full-triangle realizability using its own high-girth proof, then extracts a critical subhypergraph. Keep the containment difference and abstract-vs-actual-graph distinction. The common non-induced obstruction idea is attributed to the published work; TUZA-18’s exact induced/critical packaging has no established originality status.

## Weighted and submodular reformulations

Chapuy–DeVos–McDonald–Mohar–Scheide, *Packing Triangles in Weighted Graphs*, SIAM J. Discrete Math.28(1)(2014),226–239, DOI10.1137/100803869, https://arxiv.org/html/1012.0372 , already poses the integer-capacity weighted extension. Introducing edge capacities is therefore not a new bridge to the unweighted problem. The paper's weighted fractional inequalities do not eliminate the integral matching/covering gap. Only the relevant definitions and orientation were read; no new theorem here depends on its full proof.

Bérczi–Gehér–Imolay–Lovász–Schwarcz, *Monotonic Decompositions of Submodular Set Functions*, arXiv2406.04728v1 (7Jun2024), https://arxiv.org/html/2406.04728 , Sections2 and7–8 were read from the primary text. The published2026 version is separately listed by the institutional repository, but was not substituted silently for the inspectedv1. Its cut-decomposition norm has an upper bound m−ν* and a clique-decomposition refinement; its monotonicity comparison to maximum triangle-free subgraphs is stated as open in that version. Neither is an integral packing-to-cover transfer. The odd wheel's fractional norm is already nonintegral, and disconnected copies prevent repairing the gap merely by rounding its total value. No credible exact global implication arose from this screen; no experiment or claimed new submodular theorem was launched.
