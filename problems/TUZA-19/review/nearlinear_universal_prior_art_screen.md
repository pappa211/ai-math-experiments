## Primary comparisons

- **Zhen Liu and Qinghou Zeng, Tuza's Conjecture under an Edge-Count Condition**, [arXiv:2610.15539v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15539v1), Proposition 1.2. This is a genuine nearby equivalence: Tuza is equivalent to its validity, for each fixed c>=0 and all sufficiently large n, under e(G)>=n^2/4−cn. Its proof adds a disjoint complete bipartite graph, preserving tau and nu. It does not add universal vertices, impose TF or non-WKE links, or state an additive-error equivalence. The primary abstract and retained full-text Proposition 1.2 were checked.
- **Raphael Yuster, Dense graphs with a large triangle cover have a large triangle packing**, [arXiv:1009.0353v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.0353v1), Theorem 1.3. This records tau<=2nu+o(n^2), combining earlier mixed fractional bounds with asymptotic packing integrality. It does not specify the fixed-power-saving or subexponential rate screened here. Its primary introduction and theorem were checked. An unspecified o(n^2) error must not be promoted to the stronger rate.
- **Jacob D. Baron and Jeff Kahn, Tuza's Conjecture is Asymptotically Tight for Dense Graphs**, [arXiv:1408.4870v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.4870v2), later CPC 25 (2016), 645–667. The primary abstract gives positive-density examples making the coefficient two asymptotically sharp. This concerns ratio sharpness, not equivalence between exact Tuza and a prescribed additive rate or a universal-vertex normalization.
- **Gregory J. Puleo, Tuza's Conjecture for graphs with maximum average degree less than 7**, [primary publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2015.03.006). Its weak Konig–Egervary/reducibility framework is an acknowledged ingredient in the mission's minimal-seed preparation, rather than an originality claim of the new normalization. No statement of the new simultaneous near-linear-universal equivalence was located in the relevant source material inspected during this mission.

The previously audited Behrend/Erdos–Frankl–Rodl incidence construction remains classical and must retain its attribution. Its near-quadratic rate and linear/no-Berge-triangle properties were already checked in `review/efr_power_saving_source_audit.md`; this screen does not reclassify them as new.
