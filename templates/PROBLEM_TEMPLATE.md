# Problem identifier and short title

## Statement and source

Give the exact original statement, source URL, revision or access date, and applicable attribution/license. Define conventions and edge cases.

## Open-status check

Record the sources actually checked, the date, the result, and the limits of the search. Distinguish absence from a benchmark from a worldwide open-status or novelty claim.

## Frozen mathematical definition

Record the formal definition and alternative targets, file paths, source hashes, and dependency pins. Explain why they match the original question.

## Research sessions and model configuration

For each session: actual start/stop time and duration, objective, actual model/mode to the extent verifiable, agent roles, methods, and limitations. Exclude application-session identifiers and private operational metadata.

## Formal proof status

List declarations and their exact scopes, executed verification commands, axiom reports, and whether checks were independently repeated. Label admitted or incomplete targets clearly.

## Computational evidence

State exact or conservatively enclosed coverage, algorithms, arithmetic assumptions, completeness, source/output hashes, and resource needs. Separate finite observations from proof.

## Mathematical observations and rejected approaches

Preserve arguments, hypotheses, critical reviews, counterexamples, and limits. For a proposed route, explain its mechanism, sufficiency, difference from previous work, and smallest meaningful test.

## Reproduction

Provide portable setup and verification commands, pinned versions, actual clean-build results, remaining steps, and exclusions. Do not claim a test ran unless it did.

## Final outcome

State what is proved, what is unresolved, whether any solution candidate exists, the principal obstacle, and the recommendation. Avoid novelty claims without a separate review.
