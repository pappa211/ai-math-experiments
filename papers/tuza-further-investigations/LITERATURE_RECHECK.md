## Status and remaining bounds

No theorem in this targeted check proves general R or the proposed 5/3 strengthening. No keyword-search absence establishes novelty. The cycle deduction should be described as an ordinary consequence of color-line graphs and Brooks' theorem. Brooks' primary-paper reprint was freshly inspected in [Appendix A, pp. 537–540](https://www.tu-ilmenau.de/fileadmin/Bereiche/MN/komgra/BackMatterSMM.pdf): maximum degree at most three with no K4 component gives three-colorability, which is the exact form needed. All 2026 preprints are attributed as reported statements in the inspected versions; this check does not confer external peer review or a fresh formal verification. A current general constant cannot be described as Haxell's 66/23 alone.

Primary URL inspection is fresh as of 1 November 2026. Full proof checking was bounded to the relevant statements/mechanisms; it is not a complete specialist literature review. Access limitations are exact: Tuza full text subscription-only; some DOI redirects failed but primary author/arXiv texts were available; no third-party source was uploaded or contacted.
