I checked B-heavy's monotonicity and hereditary minimality argument: if H⊆G, a minimum-degree-five subgraph of the heavy-edge graph J(H) remains a minimum-degree-five subgraph of J(G), so U5(H)⊆U5(G); consequently H[U5(H)] is a subgraph of G[U5(G)].
