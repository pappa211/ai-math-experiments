# Mathematical inputs and exact evidence boundary

The new phase has no Lean declarations. T0 uses the unchanged public local
combined-minimality/avoidance/degree-five arguments from the
[five-degenerate paper](../../papers/tuza-five-degenerate/main.md), at their
local hypotheses. The global five-degenerate theorem is not applied to infer
mad < 7. The formal historical interface is retained at its original scope.

- [Puleo, arXiv:1308.2211v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.2211v3): the strict mad < 7
  inequality is prior work, Theorem 1.2. Reduction templates and discharging
  rules are attributed. Inequality-only irreducibility is not silently imposed
  on a combined equality obstruction. PDF SHA-256:
  `822c158830f76c61edbb3a32e308b113059318718129c6ee8380b3e91fb3d241`.
- [Botler-Fernandes-Gutierrez, arXiv:2002.07925v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.07925v2):
  reducing-triple/degree-five antecedents, especially Lemma 3.3 with its explicit
  irreducibility hypothesis. The new balanced-residual arguments supply the
  additional interface. PDF SHA-256:
  `1bb2a6b0150e25aa1da7f0d76c6950d040a486cf22f75ac6639b74d9b347d8de`.
- [Cui-Haxell-Ma](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00373-010-0881-5): known planar equality
  overlap and retained-triangle cover-saving precedent. No invention of that
  principle is claimed.
- [Lakshmanan-Bujtas-Tuza](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00373-011-1048-8): locally
  bipartite inequality antecedent, attributed through Puleo v3. The earlier
  audit could not access its original full text. That limits priority
  assessment; the present BIP proof does not depend on an inaccessible
  equality theorem.
- TUZA-11 and small-parameter 14B use [Parker v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06501v3)
  and [Liu v1.0.2 with the documented erratum](../../problems/TUZA-11/external/README.md).
  Liu's historical RUP replay is reused, not fresh here.
- TUZA-12 compares [Munaro 2018](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00373-018-1903-y), Theorem 3,
  at its K4-free and conflict restrictions. Exact K4-containing weighted-cover
  normalization is separately proved in the public archive.
- TUZA-14B fractional bounds use [Furedi 1991](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788700032699),
  equation (2.1), page 206, the published same-author restatement of the earlier
  theorem. Fano-freeness and restriction from the full LP to eligible mass are
  proved explicitly. Conditional signed-cut hypotheses remain mandatory.
- Finite bipartite matching-cover duality, Hall and Tutte-Berge are classical
  ordinary inputs at their specified finite (multi)graph hypotheses.

T0's chain is old local package -> symbolic restoration + six-link finite
classification -> low-degree/high-neighbor bounds + donor 7/8 exclusions ->
weighted discharging. SEP and the optional degree >= 10 subsumption lift are
not T0 premises. Neither S/E, Parker/Liu, Gupta's catalogue nor Kandasamy's
catalogue enters T0. No full third-party articles are redistributed.
Historical originality and external human peer review remain unestablished.
