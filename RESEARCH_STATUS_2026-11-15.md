## What is established

- A specified maximum packing whose triangles share one vertex has a cover of size at most twice its size inside its ORIGINAL support plus original private K4 completions. The common vertex need not be universal. Local 0→1, 1→2, 2→3 stability suffices; no unrestricted iteration follows.
- Every finite simple link with at most four vertices of degree at least three is WKE. Ordinary reductions plus a complete 5,375-case kernel yield some survivor R⊆G[K] with nondecreasing defect, where K is the heavy-edge five-core. The entire induced core cannot be substituted for R.
- For each fixed 1/2<a<1, Tuza is equivalent to its simultaneous universal-vertex/codegree/TF/non-WKE restriction at scale n exp(−(log n)^a). A uniform eventual error Cn² exp(−(log n)^a) implies exact Tuza. These are conditional equivalences, not a proof on the restricted class.
- The normalized class contains arbitrarily large graphs with tau<2nu<pi and ordinary ratio tending to one from below. Two-edge resilience is unestablished; H-O2 is not refuted.
- AC holds for all eligible b≤6. The first remaining conditional window is b=7,m=6,lambda=8 after a declared catalogue-completeness input. No full eligible H,X realizing it is supplied.
- Scoped square-cell, simple-root line-graph, private-tip and integral private-page theorems remain positive results with complete interfaces in the report.

The inherited TUZA-15 mad<7 equality classification remains a separate ordinary computer-assisted result; Puleo's inequality is prior work. The earlier five-degenerate Lean theorem and A113010 archive retain their original scope.

## What the obstructions mean

Fixed-palette deletion, specific compression/identification interfaces, bounded strict Hall descent, certain private-page simplifications and synchronized first marginals fail at their stated scopes. They do not show that all local algorithms, fractional tools, approximations or hierarchies fail. The connected chain admits an optimal equal-cost plateau repair. A fractional-packing gap can be offset by cover savings.

The proposed priority is global packing-support exchange; H-O2 is a secondary candidate. Neither has a supported complete solution. Publication starts no new campaign.
